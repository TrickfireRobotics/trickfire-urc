Label, Serial Hex, Serial TXT
A - 0x544e4f5246535047 - GPSFRONT
B - 0x52414552535047 - GPSREAR

To check serials you can run
udevadm info -q property -n /dev/ttyACM0 | grep ID_SERIAL
(replace ACM0 with ACM1 if testing other gps)

Sample Output:
ID_SERIAL=u-blox_AG_-_www.u-blox.com_u-blox_GNSS_receiver_GPSREAR
ID_SERIAL_SHORT=GPSREAR
ID_SERIAL=u-blox_AG_-_www.u-blox.com_u-blox_GNSS_receiver_GPSFRONT
ID_SERIAL_SHORT=GPSFRONT
